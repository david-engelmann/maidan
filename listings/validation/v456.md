## Protocol-layer properties and trust model

These are unchanged since `v441.0.0` and held by the same tests:
- nobody accepts their own approval request;
- a gate is accepted only by a signed-in person's session or `approval:grant`;
- the close gate is a durable store row, and an elicited client answer never
  decides it.

Provider-rendered approval UI is untrusted display, and the Maidan console is
the trust root. An approval whose evidence the human did not see in the console
is provisional.
