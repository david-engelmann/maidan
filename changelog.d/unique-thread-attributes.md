### One open thread per ticket: unique thread attributes

- **Added:** `GET`/`PUT /workspaces/{id}/unique-thread-attributes` (`workspace:read` to read, `token:admin` to set, audited as `workspace.unique_thread_attributes.set`) lists the task attributes no two open threads in the workspace may share a value of. `{"keys": ["linear_issue"]}` keeps one open thread per Linear ticket; `{"keys": []}`, the default, keeps nothing unique. Stored in `maidan_workspaces.unique_thread_attributes` (migration 0223 on Postgres, 0222 on SQLite).
- **Added:** With it on, creating a thread on a held ticket or setting a held ticket on an open thread is refused and writes nothing: REST `409` `conflict` with `holder_thread_id`, MCP `create_thread` and `set_thread_attributes` error `-32011` with `data.holder_thread_id`. The holder is named only to a caller who may read it. Closed, archived and erased threads hold nothing, and another workspace's threads never count. Concurrent writers of one ticket leave one winner: a transaction-scoped advisory lock per (workspace, key, value) on Postgres, the write lock on SQLite. Documented in [Swarms](docs/Swarms.md), "One owner per ticket".
