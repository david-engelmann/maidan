### Added
- The usage ledger shows the batch and flex saving (Context Economics C7c). A usage report for a call in a cheaper lane (`evidence.batch`, or a `batch` or `flex` `service_tier`) may carry `evidence.standard_price`, the model's standard-tier price snapshot. It is reporter evidence like the charged price, and Maidan keeps no discount table. A standard price on any other call is refused. The usage rollup adds `lane_reports`, `lane_usd_micros`, `lane_standard_usd_micros`, `lane_saved_usd_micros` (the standard cost less the charge, over the lane reports that carried a standard price) and `lane_unpriced_reports`. The counter `maidan_usage_lane_saved_usd_micros_total` tracks the same saving. Migration 0206 adds five nullable standard-price columns to the ledger, with no backfill: older rows count as unpriced.
