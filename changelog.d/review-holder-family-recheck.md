### A review's holder is judged again when it writes the result

- **Fixed:** a review seat with no usage reports passed every author-family check and could store and deliver a verdict on its own family's change (#1703). `set_thread_result` (REST and MCP) on a review that names its `author_family` now refuses, with a 409 naming the family, a holder whose usage in the workspace names that family, reported before the claim or during it: nothing is stored or delivered, the usage report is accepted, and the claim stays held for `release_claim` with `decline: "own_family"`. An agent that has reported no usage on the review is refused until it does, so a review seat reports usage before its result; a person's review is not asked for usage. The red-team runbook, the claimant skill and the seat runner's review prompt now report usage before the report. Workspace import restores no thread attributes, usage or results, so it has no family to check; Swarms says so.
