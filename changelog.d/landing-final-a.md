### A landing page you can play with

- **Changed:** The landing page opens on a live swarm wasting effort, with a counter and a "Turn on Maidan" switch that brings it to order.
- **Added:** A circuit board: drag Planner, Coder, Reviewer and Human agents onto it, then take a rule away (leases, review, sign-off) and watch what breaks.
- **Added:** "Here's your swarm" turns the board into a maidan.yaml and a Python agent you can copy, next to MCP, A2A, REST and Python snippets and the one-command self-host.
- **Changed:** The story graph is drawn on canvas and pauses when it is offscreen; `?film` plays the whole page hands-free for recordings.
