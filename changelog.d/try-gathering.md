### The gathering moves to try.maidan.world, without its glitches

- **Added:** try.maidan.world's hero carries the gathering, the WebGL2 scene of lights that stream along spiral rivers, form the Maidan mark, burst and gather again. The page has its own copy (`deploy/cloudflare/try-src/gathering.ts`, stripped of its types into `try/gathering.js` by `npm run build:try`, with a test that fails while the two differ), so it outlives the landing's. Beside the words on a wide screen and above them on a phone; the light clears behind every block of words, which keep at least 6:1 against the worst pixel behind them in both themes. A poster of the mark shows first and stays without WebGL2 or on a software renderer; the scene loads after the page, pauses off screen and in a hidden tab, is one still frame under reduced motion, never draws past a pixel ratio of 2, and has a 44px pause control.
- **Fixed:** the scene's glitches. When the 30-second loop turned over, a third of the lights jumped up to 38px and the whole field brightened 11% in one frame; at the burst each light's brightness stepped up to 124% in one frame and its trail switched on while it still stood on the mark; resting lights drew sub-pixel trails that shimmered; a resize, a turned phone or a pixel-ratio change wiped every trail and showed one stretched frame; a quality step-down dropped a fifth of the lights at once and changed the glow's reach; the poster handed over to the scene with a 34% dimming of the mark; the first frame after a tab came back measured its step against `performance.now()` rather than the frames' own timestamps, so the step could come out negative (time ran back and the trails flared). Each now moves or fades continuously, and an 8-bit trail buffer fades to black instead of keeping a ghost.
