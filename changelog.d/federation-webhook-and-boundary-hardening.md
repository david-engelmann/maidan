### Fixed

- **Fixed:** Cross-tenant channel event visibility is denied before membership checks, and long-lived visibility caches are bounded. Peer/webhook AEAD secrets reject oversized plaintext and ciphertext and require a full nonce+tag frame, short-circuiting invalid ciphertext before key rotation. Federation ingest verifies then parses before advancing the verified-link pointer, records the link only after a successful parse (or publish), and idempotently replays an already-verified envelope so a mixed batch cannot wedge on retry; nested channel remaps stay workspace-local; federation and webhook env knobs trim and clamp. Deleted or revoked webhooks quarantine queued deliveries without firing HTTP or re-caching secrets, and undecryptable secrets advance attempts instead of head-of-line stalling. DNS-rebinding refuses duplicate/non-UTF-8 Host and Origin, hardens IPv6 host parsing, and treats empty Authorization as unauthenticated; egress DNS pinning covers trailing-dot FQDNs; Slack bodies refuse pipe/angle backlinks, stay under the size ceiling, and do not split HTML entities; secret-egress hosts normalize trailing dots; `X-Request-Id` accepts only safe trimmed ids. OTLP endpoint/service/interval config trims and clamps, `Guard::with_meter` attaches metrics for shutdown, and W3C `tracestate` is preserved when adopting a remote parent.
