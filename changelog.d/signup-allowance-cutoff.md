### Open sign-up workspaces are cut off past their free allowance

- **Added:** a workspace records its origin (`operator`, `signup` or `open`; migration 0209). A workspace created by open sign-up (`MAIDAN_SIGNUP_OPEN`) that is marked past its free allowance is refused that meter's work for the rest of the month: requests answer `429 Too Many Requests` (its usage and notifications stay readable), MCP tool calls answer JSON-RPC `-32030`, semantic and hybrid search and the embedding of new messages stop on `embedding_calls`, egress (Slack, GitHub, Linear), mail and web push stop on `deliveries` (queued work is parked, not failed), WASI slash handlers do not run on `wasi_ms`, and posting messages and uploading artifacts are refused on `stored_bytes`. The existing crossing notice tells its admins. Operator-created and invited workspaces stay mark-only. The cutoff is reloaded on every replica each flush tick, so it can trail the crossing by 1–2 intervals.
