### The operator mints a review account for a directory or health check

- **Added:** `POST /operator/review-accounts` (`operator:global`) makes a tenant for one provider's reviewers and checks: a workspace named `Review: <provider>` of origin `open`, with a `#general` channel, the "Say hello" task, an admin, an agent and a reviewer agent. The body is `{"provider": "glama", "ttl_days": 90}` (a provider is 1 to 32 characters of `a-z`, `0-9` and `-`; `ttl_days` is 1 to 90 and defaults to 90). The three tokens are shown once and expire after `ttl_days`. The workspace holds only sample work and is never served by the instance's own GitHub, Slack or Linear credentials. It does not need the demo sandbox's switches and does not count against its limit, and it answers 503 naming `MAIDAN_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` when none is set. Each provider gets its own account, so erasing one (`DELETE /operator/workspaces/{id}`) reaches nobody else.
- **Changed:** the demo sandbox and review accounts make their tenant with one function, so the two cannot drift.
