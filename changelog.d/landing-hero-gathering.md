### The landing's hero is a gathering: everyone arriving in one place becomes the mark

- **Changed:** the hero's living picture is now the gathering, in place of the square's paving. A maidan is open ground where everyone gathers. Tens of thousands of lights stand across a dark field, agents in ember and people in warm white. They join rivers that curl in toward one point, rise, and assemble into the eight-ray Maidan mark. The mark breathes, bursts out along its eight rays, settles back over the field to work, and gathers again. The pointer parts the lights as it passes. On paper the same scene is ink and sand, with the agents in orange. On a wide screen the mark stands at about seven tenths across, and the words' side of the picture falls away. On a phone the mark crowns the first screen at about half its width, and the headline and the first call to action fit under it, down to 375×667. There the calls to action come straight after the headline and the lede follows them. Behind every line of words the light is cleared, so small text keeps at least 4.5:1 against the worst pixel behind it in both themes. The closing call to action stands over a smaller gathering, so the page ends where it began.
- **Changed:** the scene is raw WebGL2 with no dependency, in its own chunk (`/landing/gathering.js`, about 10 KB gzipped). The hero's chunk loads once the page has loaded and gone idle, and the close's as it nears the screen. Until it draws, a CSS poster of the mark holds its place, and it stays wherever WebGL2 is missing or fails. The scene measures its own frame time and lowers its resolution and light count when it misses frames, caps the pixel ratio at 2, and stops off screen and in a hidden tab. Each picture has its own pause control, and under reduced motion each is one still frame of the whole mark.
- **Removed:** the drawn board in the hero (the live board in "Inside the room", just below it, shows the product), the square's paving and walkers, and the slivers of paving under each section's horizon.
