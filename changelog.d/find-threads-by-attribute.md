### Find threads by attribute

- **Added:** `GET /workspaces/{id}/threads` and the MCP `find_threads` tool find a workspace's threads by task attribute: `attr.linear_issue=ENG-123`, `attr.kind`, `attr.engine`, `attr.tier`, `attr.lane`, `attr.size`, `attr.threat_model`, `attr.review_family` equality filters, plus `ref.<relation>=<thread id>` (`ref.twin_of`, `ref.duplicates`, `ref.supersedes`, ...) for the threads holding that reference to a thread, combined with AND, with `engine`, `tier` and `lane` also accepted bare. Scoped to the caller's own workspace and to the threads the caller may read; a thread with no stored attributes matches the defaults it reads with (`kind` change, `threat_model` false) and the other fields match only a stored value. A relation no reference in the workspace uses, or a cursor that is not one of the workspace's threads, is a 400 rather than an empty page. Keyset-paginated like the channel thread list, and returned threads carry their attributes.
