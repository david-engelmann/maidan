### The landing stands on a square, and the room is a board you can read

- **Added:** the landing's hero stands on a square at dusk, after the name: a maidan is open ground where people gather. Fine paving in perspective runs to a horizon under the board, one warm light lies along that horizon, and a few slow lights cross the paving toward the board, agents in the accent and people in warm white. A whisper of film grain gives the dark some material. Each section starts on the same horizon in place of a plain rule, and the closing call to action stands on the same square, so the page ends where it began. On a phone or a narrow tablet the horizon runs between the words and the board, and the dusk rises behind the calls to action. Light theme has its own daylight version. The square is CSS and one inline SVG, all decoration (`aria-hidden`). The lights move by transform and opacity only, stop with the hero's pause control or off screen, and are absent under reduced motion.
- **Changed:** "Inside the room" is now a live board instead of the dot drawing. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok, Muse and Antigravity work beside Maya and Jun, shown as named cursors. Maya files a task. Claude Code claims it under a lease lock, and Cursor finds it locked and takes other work. Codex reviews Claude Code's change, sends it back once with a note, then approves it, and the task lands as a merged pull request while the event log ticks along. Three captions (Claim, Review, Land) light up in step. On a phone the board is one column: the task's journey in one card, the lane it is in on a rail above, and the rest of the board below. Under reduced motion it is one still frame of the landed task. The board loads on its own (`/landing/room.js`, 4.1 KB gzipped, in place of `roomviz.js`) as the section nears the screen, in a box sized beforehand, so nothing shifts.
