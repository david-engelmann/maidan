# Brand artwork sources

These SVGs are local copies of the original artwork, copied unchanged from
Stageshow's `kit/brands/` (commit `299140fa`), which lists the same sources.
Their colors and proportions are unchanged.

- [SVGL](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl), MIT. See `LICENSE-SVGL.txt`.
- [Linear brand assets](https://linear.app/brand): official light and dark
  logomarks, downloaded 25 October 2026. Subject to Linear’s published brand
  usage terms.

The asset repositories’ software licenses do not confer ownership of the
brands. These marks identify the respective products; follow their brand
guidelines for the intended use.
