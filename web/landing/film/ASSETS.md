# Assets

The film's scene is generated by the scripts in this folder. It uses a few
scanned assets, none of which are in the repository: `fetch_assets.py`
downloads them. All are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Asset | Kind | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `studio_small_03` | Studio environment, 2K HDR | Reflections and faint fill in the dark set-up | <https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_03> |
| `studio_kontrast_04` | Studio environment, 2K HDR | Reflections and fill in the light set-up | <https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_kontrast_04> |
| `white_maple_veneer` | Scanned wood, 2K (colour, roughness, displacement) | The maple blocks, plates, park panel and beads | <https://polyhaven.com/a/white_maple_veneer> |
| `ash_veneer` | Scanned wood, 2K (colour, roughness, displacement) | The ash blocks | <https://polyhaven.com/a/ash_veneer> |

Everything else (brass, acrylic, felt, resin, the charcoal board, the
studio's table and sweep, the laser engraving on the park panel) is made in
the scripts.

No add-ons, `.blend` files or models are downloaded.
